## Defect fixed

A paused session allowed Arm and adapter switching. Connecting hardware during a paused simulation could enter Resume without the runtime Home requirement. Paused sessions now block Arm, Connect and Home until Stop; Resume explicitly requires valid motor homing. Automatic adapter connection also skips paused sessions.

## Planner performance

Offline sweeps used the recent measured working span (143962 encoder counts), the new 180 mm/s and 360 mm/s² budgets, 20 ms engine steps, 60 seconds per waveform, and excluded the first 10 seconds. These measure requested versus planned command, not physical tracking or serial throughput.

| Sine rate | Stroke | RMS command error, percentage points of working span |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.25 Hz | 70% | 2.63 |
| 0.5 Hz | 30% | 1.81 |
| 1 Hz | 10% | 0.76 |
| 0.5 Hz | 70% | 31.85 |
| 1 Hz | 70% | 24.34 |

At 1 Hz / 70% stroke, requested span was 56% of the working range but planned span was only 13.9%. Raising Rate does not guarantee faster full strokes. Wide high-rate patterns exceed the acceleration budget and can lose waveform fidelity severely. Saw and square discontinuities are also heavily limited. Bounds and velocity/acceleration ceilings held throughout the sweeps.
