## Defect fixed

A paused session allowed Arm and adapter switching. Connecting hardware during a paused simulation could enter Resume without the runtime Home requirement. Paused sessions now block Arm, Connect and Home until Stop; Resume explicitly requires valid motor homing. Automatic adapter connection also skips paused sessions.

ramps the trajectory. The upstream library uses dedicated absolute-position
function 0x7b. On the connected motor, inhibited-output probes established that
its reply carries the actual encoder position, rather than echoing the target.

- Raising the drive ramp to 20000 RPM/s while keeping it enabled, with a 600 RPM
