## Defect fixed

A paused session allowed Arm and adapter switching. Connecting hardware during a paused simulation could enter Resume without the runtime Home requirement. Paused sessions now block Arm, Connect and Home until Stop; Resume explicitly requires valid motor homing. Automatic adapter connection also skips paused sessions.

## Planner performance

Offline sweeps used the recent measured working span (143962 encoder counts), the new 180 mm/s and 360 mm/s² budgets, 20 ms engine steps, 60 seconds per waveform, and excluded the first 10 seconds. These measure requested versus planned command, not physical tracking or serial throughput.

| Sine rate | Stroke | RMS command error, percentage points of working span |

At 1 Hz / 70% stroke, requested span was 56% of the working range but planned span was only 13.9%. Raising Rate does not guarantee faster full strokes. Wide high-rate patterns exceed the acceleration budget and can lose waveform fidelity severely. Saw and square discontinuities are also heavily limited. Bounds and velocity/acceleration ceilings held throughout the sweeps.

## Reconnected motor follow-up

Tests used the production JavaScript runtime, Web Serial framing and MotorTransport,
with a local serial pipe to the actual adapter. Chrome's chooser remained empty
while direct serial access worked; these tests do not sign off Chrome selection
or browser scheduling. Raw motor logs and samples remain local.

- Three complete Home sequences measured 179.7 mm, with endpoint differences of only four encoder counts across sequences. Center parking error was below 0.02 mm; output was confirmed inhibited.
- 0.25 Hz / 70% sine ran for 25 seconds, past the former 20-second limit. Encoder span was 98.3 mm versus approximately 98.4 mm requested. Pause verified stop, froze phase, and Resume succeeded. Changing to 0.5 Hz / 30% triangle succeeded; final stop was confirmed. Two communication recoveries occurred without ending the sequence.
- 0.5 Hz / 70% sine failed with the existing three-sample tracking-error fault. Cleanup confirmed disabled output, zero pending motion and zero PWM. This operating point is not signed off.
- 1 Hz / 10% sine ran for 15 seconds with combined Play, Pause/Resume, then a 0.5 Hz / 30% triangle change. Final stop was confirmed, with no faults or communication recoveries. Encoder span was 13.8 mm versus 14.0 mm commanded.
- Same-time encoder-to-command RMS differences were approximately 12.1 mm for the broad slow sine and 5.9 mm for the fast short sine. Feedback age contributes to these differences, but substantial phase lag remains; matching travel span does not establish waveform fidelity.
- Final drive state: stationary feedback, output disabled, pending zero, PWM zero, alarm zero. Port released.

The controls now use Connect → Home → Play, with arming preflight inside Play,
Pause/Resume on the same button, and a separate Stop. 147 browser tests pass.


## Faster streaming follow-up

The drive manual and upstream motor library were inspected locally. The manual
explicitly describes lag from internal ramps when an external controller already
ramps the trajectory. The upstream library uses dedicated absolute-position
function 0x7b. On the connected motor, inhibited-output probes established that
its reply carries the actual encoder position, rather than echoing the target.

- Raising the drive ramp to 20000 RPM/s while keeping it enabled, with a 600 RPM
  speed ceiling, made the formerly failing 0.5 Hz / 70% test complete. Encoder
  travel was 97.1 mm. Pause/Resume and final stop passed, without communication
  recoveries.
- The dedicated stream uses 33 ms requested updates, per-reply encoder feedback,
  and full status snapshots every 100 ms. The planner acceleration was then
  raised to 2160 mm/s², with 360 mm/s velocity headroom.
- The 0.5 Hz / 70% stream covered 97.8 mm versus 98.0 mm commanded.
- A 15-second 1 Hz / 70% stream covered 98.1 mm versus 98.4 mm commanded and
  ended with confirmed disabled output, zero pending motion, speed and PWM,
  no alarm, and no communication recoveries. Pause/Resume also passed.
- Same-time command/readback RMS differences remain about 12.0 mm at 0.5 Hz
  and 22.2 mm at 1 Hz. Full travel at higher rate is validated; phase fidelity
  is still a limitation. These observations supersede the earlier failed
  operating point only for this tested streaming profile.
- Tests ran through the production JavaScript runtime and transport using a local
  serial pipe. Chrome enumeration and browser-background scheduling remain
  separate unresolved validation items. Raw telemetry remains local.
