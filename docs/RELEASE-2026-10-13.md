# Browser motion release — October 13, 2026

The continuous motion planner and completion-driven serial path are published
at [OSSM Motion Synth](https://lucychapar.com/ossm-motion-synth/). The connected
OSSM has already been commissioned to 115200 baud. Connect detects its working
baud without changing persistent settings; Home establishes the rail and
applies the 1800 RPM / 50000 RPM/s `servo_ramped` profile. Home and Play remain
explicit actions. No firmware flash is required.

During the published-browser release check, a 1.5 Hz / 70% sine exceeded the
following-error fence. That broad-pattern run did not pass. This occurred on
`85b678b`, before the final Stop-display correction; the hardware planner,
runtime, transport and I/O modules are identical in the released revision.
Three subsequent
independent CRC-valid read-only samples showed identical encoder position and
zero output, pending demand, PWM, speed and alarm. They establish inhibited,
stationary hardware; the separate probe did not produce a transport Stop
confirmation. The final UI correction keeps a verified Stop visible while a
motion fault remains latched. It changes no motion limit or fault threshold.

The exact released app subsequently passed a fresh Home at approximately
179.7 nominal mm and a short, bounded **2.5 Hz / 30% sine**, centered at 50%,
with limits of 10–90% and no patch cables. The UI displayed approximately
42.2 nominal mm of requested travel. RUNNING and encoder feedback were observed
without a guard fault. The manual trial lasted approximately six seconds;
its exact duration was not instrumented. The UI showed 70 command updates/s
while running and retained 73/s after **Stop → Confirmed**. These are rounded
one-second reply estimates, not whole-run averages.

The machine was left connected at 115200 baud, homed and stopped, with Play
enabled and those sine settings retained. The [published-app screenshot](images/published-motion-2026-10-13.jpg)
records the final state. Human smoothness acceptance was not recorded for this
published trial.

The earlier [serial throughput audit](SERIAL_THROUGHPUT_AUDIT.md) and
[QA record](qa/2026-10-13-serial-throughput.json) retain the local harness and
localhost results for their exact revisions. Those results are not a passing
result for the broader published-browser run. This release has no fixed 20/50 Hz
target cadence cap; 500 Hz was not achieved on the connected path. The physical
checks do not establish general smoothness across patterns or parity with stock
firmware.
